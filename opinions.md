# My Opinions and Values

## Artificial Intelligence
I think Artificial Intelligence is one of the most useful technologies for solving practical problems. I like AI most when it is used for something real, such as healthcare screening or workplace safety, which are the areas of my own projects.

I prefer learning AI through practical projects instead of only studying theory. Reading the theory helps, but I only feel I understand a concept when I have used it in a project.

## Programming
I believe programming becomes easier when I practice by building actual projects. Watching or reading is not enough for me. When I build something, the concepts start to make sense.

## Learning
I prefer step-by-step explanations when learning a new technical topic. If someone explains things in small steps, I can follow them and then try the next step myself.

## Projects
I prefer projects that solve a real problem rather than projects that only demonstrate theory. That is why I am happy to work on ECHO TB, where the goal is to help with early tuberculosis screening in places with limited healthcare access.

## My Work Style
I like breaking a large task into smaller steps. A big task can look scary, but small tasks feel manageable.

When I do not understand something, I prefer to understand the basic concept first and then implement it. I do not like to copy something that I do not understand.

## Online selling and digital products
I am interested in online selling and digital products. Because I already run a small manufacturing and e-commerce business, I find this area exciting and practical.

## Coffee
I drink a lot of coffee. People say it is too much, but it is part of how I work and study.
