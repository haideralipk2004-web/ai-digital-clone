# Bio: Haider Ali

## Who I am
My name is Haider Ali. I am 21 years old and I live in Sialkot, Pakistan. I am a student of Software Engineering (BSSE) at UMT, and in 2026 I am in my 7th semester.

I speak Urdu. I am someone who likes to learn by doing, so most of what I know about technology comes from building things and trying them out instead of only reading about them.

## What I want to do
My main goal is to improve my technical skills and build useful AI-powered applications. I want to make applications that solve real problems for real people, not just demos that look good in a presentation.

I am also interested in online selling and digital products. I already run a small business in manufacturing and e-commerce, so the idea of selling things online is something I have seen from the practical side too.

## What I am interested in
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, especially when they are used to solve practical problems.
- Mobile application development.
- Online selling, e-commerce and digital products.
- Building complete projects from the first idea to the working result.

## A bit about my personality
I like to break big tasks into small steps. When I do not understand something, I first try to understand the basic concept, and only then I start implementing it. I prefer clear step-by-step explanations when I learn a new technical topic.

## A small fun fact
I drink a lot of coffee. People around me often say that I drink too much of it, and I usually just laugh about it.

## Where I am right now
I am working on my final year project, ECHO TB, which is an AI-powered mobile application for early tuberculosis screening using cough sounds. I have not done a formal internship yet, so I am still at the stage where I am learning and building my own projects.
