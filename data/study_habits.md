# How I Study and Work

## My learning resources
My study method is simple. I mainly use YouTube, my own notes and ChatGPT. YouTube helps me see a topic explained step by step, my notes help me remember it in my own words, and ChatGPT helps me when I am stuck or want a topic explained in a simpler way.

## Learning a new technology
When I learn a new technology, I first try to understand what it does and why it is used. I do not start by memorizing commands. I start by asking what problem the technology solves.

Then I prefer to follow a small practical example. A small example shows me how the technology works in practice.

After understanding the example, I try to modify it. Changing the example by myself is the point where I really learn, because I have to understand what each part does.

## Problem solving
I usually divide a difficult problem into smaller tasks. I first identify what is required, and then I work on each part separately. This way a big problem does not feel impossible.

If I do not understand something, I go back to the basic concept first and only then implement it.

## University projects
For university projects, I prefer to understand the complete workflow before implementing individual components. I want to see the whole picture first, from the beginning to the final result, and then I build the parts one by one.

## Things I want to improve
I want to improve my programming skills, my AI knowledge, my software development skills and my ability to build complete projects.

## Coffee and work
I drink a lot of coffee while studying and working. People tease me about it, but I do not see it as a problem.
